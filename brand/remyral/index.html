05 / IDENTITY GUIDE

Brand usage guide

Temel kurallar

Keep the ratio between the symbol and the wordmark. Do not stretch or rotate the logo. Prefer the navy variant on light backgrounds and the white variant on dark ones. At very small sizes use the symbol alone. Show the full-colour logo on neutral backgrounds where possible.

Minimum size: horizontal logo 120 px wide, symbol 24 px. Leave clear space around the symbol of at least half its lobe width.
